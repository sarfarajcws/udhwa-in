In a major push towards digital education, the Jharkhand government has launched the ICT Championship eShiksha Mahotsav 2025, a state-wide competition that promises to transform how students in government schools engage with technology.

## The Digital Revolution in Classrooms

When I visited a government school in Ranchi yesterday, the excitement was palpable. Students were huddled around computers, practicing for the upcoming competition.

'This is the first time I'm getting a chance to show what I can do with computers,'
said 15-year-old Priya Kumari from Class 10, her eyes sparkling with excitement.

## Four-Level Competition Structure

The championship follows a carefully designed four-level structure:

• School Level (September 23-27, 2025) - Bronze medals and certificates • Block Level (November 3-8, 2025) - Silver medals and school bags • District Level (November 13-19, 2025) - Gold medals and smartwatches • State Level (November 24-29, 2025) - Tablets and laptops for winners

## Real Impact on Ground

Mr. Rajesh Sharma, a computer teacher at a government school in Sahibganj, shared:

'For many of our students, this competition is not just about winning prizes. It's about gaining confidence and realizing that they can compete with anyone in the digital world.'

## Government's Vision

The initiative, organized by the Jharkhand Education Project Council, aligns with the National Education Policy 2020's focus on digital literacy.

'We're preparing our students for the future, not just for exams,'
said an education department official.

As I left the school, I saw groups of students discussing coding and digital presentations. The ICT Championship is clearly more than a competition - it's sparking a digital revolution in Jharkhand's classrooms.
