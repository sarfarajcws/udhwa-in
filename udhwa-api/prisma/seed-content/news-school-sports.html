+2 High School Udhwa is organizing its Annual Sports Meet 2026 for students. The event includes various track and field competitions and aims to encourage sportsmanship, discipline, and physical fitness among students.

📢 Important Notice

This information is shared for public awareness based on the official school notice/poster. All students are encouraged to participate and showcase their athletic talents.

🏅 Events & Competitions

The Annual Sports Meet 2026 will feature a wide range of track and field events suitable for different age groups and skill levels:

  • 100m Race
  • 200m Race
  • 400m Race
  • 1500m Race
  • Relay Race
  • Discus Throw
  • Javelin Throw
  • Shot Put
  • High Jump
  • Long Jump
  • Triple Jump
  • Musical Chairs (Girls Only)

👥 Age Categories

  • Up to 14 Years — Junior Category For younger students with age-appropriate competitions
  • Above 14 Years — Senior Category For senior students with advanced level competitions

📜 Important Rules & Regulations

To ensure fair play and smooth conduct of the sports meet, the following rules must be followed:

  • Participants must be students of +2 High School, Udhwa.
  • A participant can participate in a maximum of three events.
  • Each participant must bring their identity card for verification.
  • Indiscipline or unsportsmanlike behavior will result in immediate disqualification.

📅 Important Dates

📝 Entry & Registration

Dates: 9, 10, 15, 16 January 2026

Time: School hours (10:00 AM - 3:00 PM)

Venue: School Office

⚠️ Late entries will not be accepted

🏆 Final Competition

Dates: 19 – 20 January 2026

Time: 9:00 AM onwards

Venue: School Ground

🎯 Objectives of the Sports Meet

  • To promote physical fitness and healthy lifestyle among students
  • To identify and nurture sports talent in the school
  • To develop team spirit and sportsmanship
  • To provide a platform for healthy competition
  • To encourage discipline and time management
